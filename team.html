Jacqueline Robinson

Founder — Forethought Care & Support

Bio

Jacqueline Robinson brings more than five decades of experience across mental health, research governance, ethics, and professional education. Her career spans Australia and Canada, with roles ranging from forensic psychiatric nursing and community mental health to senior ethics, governance, and compliance leadership across major health and university sectors.

Jacqueline has held key positions, including Research Governance Officer at the Royal Brisbane and Women’s Hospital, Ethics Advisor at the Queensland Brain Institute, and Research Governance Officer & Contract Support at the Queensland Cerebral Palsy and Rehabilitation Research Centre. Her work has consistently centred on safeguarding ethical practice, strengthening governance systems, and supporting researchers and clinicians to meet national and international standards. “Provide Ethics and Governance compliance assessments and training”

As an educator, Jacqueline has taught postgraduate mental health programs at the University of Queensland, developed statewide mental health training, and delivered hundreds of workshops on ethics, boundaries, crisis intervention, recovery‑oriented practice, and professional communication. “Development of integrated specialist mental health education programs… design, delivery and evaluation”

She is also an experienced clinical supervisor, supporting mental health professionals in ethical decision‑making, reflective practice, and recovery‑aligned care. “Supervision of competency and applicable ethical professional mental health care practice”

Areas of Contribution

  • Ethics and governance guidance for clinicians, researchers, and organisations
  • Professional boundaries education for psychologists and allied health teams
  • Clinical and professional supervision
  • Training design and delivery across mental health, wellbeing, and ethical practice
  • Risk, compliance, and documentation support for practice owners and research teams

  • Registered Nurse (RN) — AHPRA
